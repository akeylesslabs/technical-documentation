Email event notifications can now be customized to match your organization's branding and communication preferences. You can configure the **sender informatio**n, **email subject**, **account details** displayed in the email, **footer**, and **images**.

<Callout icon="📘" theme="warn">
  ### Note

  The Email template applies to event emails only. Other notifications are not affected.
</Callout>

# Configuration

To customize the email template for event notifications:

1. Go to **Account Settings** > **Email Customization**.
2. Configure the following fields:
   - **Sender Name**: The name displayed as the sender of the email, by default - **Akeyless Security**.
   - **Email Subject:&#x20;**&#x54;he subject of the email, by default - **Security Notice: Your attention is required**.
   - **Account Information**: Select which account details to include in the email body. Available options include:
     - **Account ID**
     - **Company Name**
     - **Tenant Name**
     - **Account Alias**
   - **Email Footer**: Add a custom footer that will appear at the bottom of the email.
   - **Email Image**: Choose whether to include images in the email body.

# Send a Test Email

Before saving your changes, you can send a test email to preview the template.

Click **Send a Test Email** to send a test email to the email address of the user currently configuring the template. Review the email to verify the content, branding, and layout before saving your changes.

# Reset to Default

After saving the template, you can restore the default email template at any time by clicking **Reset to Default.**

All customized fields will be reverted to their default values.
