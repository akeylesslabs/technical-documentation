Inventory can be used to review **secret**, **identity**, and **certificate** findings, filtering by **type**, **status**, and **severity**, drilling into finding details, and updating finding status.

## Prerequisites

Inventory is shown only when:

- The account has the Identity and Secrets Intelligence feature enabled.
- The user has admin-level Console access, or a role with the `isi-access` rule set to `scoped` or `all`.

For full RBAC setup instructions, see [Control Access With RBAC](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence#control-access-with-role-based-access-control-rbac).

## Using Inventory

1. Sign in to the Akeyless Console.
2. In the left navigation, open **Identity & Secrets Intelligence**.
3. Select **Inventory**.
4. Filter findings by type, status, and severity.
5. Open a finding to review its details.
6. Update the finding's status as needed.

You can also reach Inventory directly from a running scan in [**Scanners**](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence-scanners), to jump straight from a scan to its resulting findings.

## Where Findings Come From

Findings in Inventory are generated by scans run from [**Scanners**](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence-scanners), across any of the supported scanner types (**AWS**, **Akeyless**, **Azure**, **GCP**, **GitHub**, and **Kubernetes**). See [Scanner Types](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence-scanners#scanner-types) for details on each source type and its required permissions.
