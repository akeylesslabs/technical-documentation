Inventory can be used to review **secret**, **identity**, and **certificate** findings, filtering by **type**, **status**, and **severity**, drilling into finding details, and updating finding status.

## Prerequisites

Inventory is shown only when:

- The account has the Identity and Secrets Intelligence feature enabled.
- The user has admin-level Console access, or a role with the `isi-access` rule set to `scoped` or `all`.

For full RBAC setup instructions, see [Control Access With RBAC](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence#control-access-with-role-based-access-control-rbac).

## Using Inventory

1. Sign in to the Akeyless Console.
2. In the left navigation, open **Identity & Secrets Intelligence**.
3. Select **Inventory**.
4. Filter findings by type, status, and severity.
5. Open a finding to review its details.
6. Update the finding's status as needed.

You can also reach Inventory directly from a running scan in [**Scanners**](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence-scanners), to jump straight from a scan to its resulting findings.

## Where Findings Come From

Findings in Inventory are generated by scans run from [**Scanners**](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence-scanners), across any of the supported scanner types (**AWS**, **Akeyless**, **Azure**, **GCP**, **GitHub**, and **Kubernetes**). See [Scanner Types](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence-scanners#scanner-types) for details on each source type and its required permissions.<br /><br /><br /><br /><br />

# Inventory

Inventory is where every finding Identity & Secrets Intelligence surfaces — across secrets, identities, and certificates — lives. Each finding is filterable by type, status, and severity, and traces back to a policy defined in Secret, Identity, or Certificate Policies.

## Prerequisites

- The account has the Identity and Secrets Intelligence feature enabled.
- The user has admin-level Console access, or a role with the `isi-access` rule set to `scoped` or `all`.

For full RBAC setup instructions, see [Control Access With RBAC](doc:identity-and-secrets-intelligence#control-access-with-role-based-access-control-rbac).

## The Findings List

The Inventory screen lists every finding as a row. Each row shows:

- **Name** — the flagged secret, identity, or certificate
- **Policy** — the Secret, Identity, or Certificate Policy the finding violates
- **Scanner** — the scanner that discovered the finding
- **Source** — the connected account or environment the finding came from
- **Status** — Open, or a status you've set, such as Ignored or Risk Accepted
- **Type** — Secret, Identity, or Certificate
- **Severity** — Critical, High, Medium, or Low

Use the filter icon to narrow the list to Secrets, Identities, or Certificates, or sort by Severity to prioritize what needs attention first.

## Reviewing a Finding

Selecting a finding opens its detail view, showing three tabs.

### Overview

The Overview tab explains the finding in plain language — what was detected, why it matters, and any relevant context Identity & Secrets Intelligence surfaced from the underlying source.

### Security Graph

The Security Graph tab shows a graph of the roles, identities, and resources connected to this finding, so you can see what's related to it and understand its context before acting.

### Remediate

The Remediate tab lists numbered remediation steps for the finding, along with AI-powered remediation assistance you can use to get more detail. From here, you can also mark a finding as **Ignore** or **Accept Risk** instead of remediating it.

## Using Inventory

To review and act on findings:

1. Log in to the Akeyless Console, and go to **Identity & Secrets Intelligence**.
2. Select **Inventory**.
3. Filter or search the findings list.
4. Select a finding to review its Overview, Security Graph, and Remediate details.
