## Download

| Platform | Store listing | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **iPhone** | [Akeyless Password Manager on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/akeyless-password-manager/id6821884962) | iOS 15.0 or later. iPhone only |
| **Android** | [Akeyless Password Manager on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akeyless.passwordmanager) | Android 7.0 or later. AutoFill needs Android 8.0 or later |

Both listings are published by **Akeyless Security** and are free. Store installs update
themselves, so you always have the latest fixes.

<Callout icon="⚠️" theme="warn">
  Use the links on this page rather than searching the store. Search results can also show
  other Akeyless apps, such as the Secure Remote Access apps, which are different products.
  The listing you want is called **Akeyless Password Manager**.
</Callout>

## Install

### iPhone

1. Open [the App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/akeyless-password-manager/id6821884962) on your iPhone.
2. Tap **Get**, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your Apple Account password.
3. Open **Akeyless Password Manager** from your Home Screen.

### Android

1. Open [the Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.akeyless.passwordmanager) on your phone.
2. Tap **Install**.
3. Tap **Open**, or open **Akeyless Password Manager** from your app drawer.

## Before you sign in

You need an existing Akeyless account. The app has no registration screen. If you do not
have an account yet, create one at
[console.akeyless.io/registration](https://console.akeyless.io/registration), or ask your
administrator which sign-in method your organization uses.

Have these ready:

| You sign in with | You need |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Your email address, password, and the environment your account is hosted in |
| **Alias** | Your alias |
| **SAML** or **OIDC** | Your organization's Access ID |
| **Access ID** | Your Access ID and Access Key |

Then continue to [Signing In on Mobile](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-mobile-sign-in).

## After signing in

Two things are worth setting up right away:

1. **[AutoFill](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-mobile-autofill)** — so your saved logins
   fill into Safari, Chrome and other apps.
2. **[Favorites](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/adding-password-to-favorites-1)** — star
   the items you use most, so they sit one tap away on the **Favorites** tab.

## Updating

The stores update the app automatically. To update by hand, open the store listing and tap
**Update**. Your vault lives in Akeyless, not on the phone, so updating or reinstalling
never loses items — you only need to sign in again after a reinstall.
