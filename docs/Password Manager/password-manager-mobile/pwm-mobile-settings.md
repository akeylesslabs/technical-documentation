Open settings with **More** → **Settings**.

## What is on the screen

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Account** | At the top: who you are signed in as |
| **Dark Mode** | Switches the app between light and dark. Your choice is kept after you sign out. Hidden when your organization's branding sets the theme |
| **File storage** | How much of your account's file storage quota is used and how much remains |
| **Contact Support** | Opens your organization's support page, or Akeyless support |
| **Privacy Policy** | Opens your organization's privacy policy, or the Akeyless one |
| **Version** | The app version — include it when you contact support |
| **Sign out** | Signs you out and removes your saved logins from the phone's AutoFill |

## Settings that live elsewhere on mobile

Some browser extension settings do not apply to a phone, so the mobile app does not show
them:

| In the extension | On mobile |
|---|---|
| **Autofill** toggle | Turned on in your phone's settings instead — see [AutoFill on iPhone and Android](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-mobile-autofill) |
| **Prompt to save password** | Not available. Create the item in the app |
| **AI agent auto-fill** | Not available |
| **Import Passwords** | Use the [web extension](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/csv-password-importer) or the [web console](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-console-import) |
| **Export Passwords** | Use the [web extension](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/web-extension-export-passwords) |

Camera access for QR scanning is managed in your phone's settings — see
[One-Time Passwords: Scanning a QR Code](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-mobile-otp#camera-permission).
