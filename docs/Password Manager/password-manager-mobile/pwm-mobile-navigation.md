## The tab bar

The tab bar along the bottom of the screen takes you between the areas of your vault. It
shrinks while you scroll, to give the list more room.

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Personal** | Your own items, visible only to you — see [Personal Secrets](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/web-extension-personal-area) |
| **Corporate** | Items shared with you through your organization's access roles — see [Corporate Secrets](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/web-extension-corporate-area) |
| **Favorites** | The items and folders you starred, from both areas — see [Adding Items To Favorites](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/adding-password-to-favorites-1) |
| **Health** | The Security Health score for your personal passwords — see [Security Health in the Extension](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/web-extension-security-health) |
| **More** | Opens a sheet with **Recycle Bin**, **Settings** and **Web Console** |

<Callout icon="ℹ️" theme="info">
  **A tab is missing?** Tabs follow your account. **Personal** and **Health** are hidden when
  your account has no personal vault — including whenever you sign in with **Access ID** — and
  **Corporate** is hidden when your access roles do not allow the corporate vault. Your
  administrator controls both.
</Callout>

### The More sheet

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Recycle Bin** | Deleted items, ready to restore or delete forever — see [Recycle Bin](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/web-extension-recycle-bin) |
| **Settings** | App settings and **Sign out** — see [Mobile Settings](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-mobile-settings) |
| **Web Console** | Opens the Akeyless web console for your account |

Tap **Cancel**, tap outside the sheet, or drag it down to close it. Every bottom sheet in the
app closes the same way.

## The header

Each area has a large title, a search field and a row of tools. Which tools appear depends on
the area — **View** and **+** only appear in Personal and Corporate:

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sort** | Toggles between **A–Z** and **Z–A** |
| **Filter** | Opens a filter sheet with **Types**, **Tags** and, on Favorites, **Location** (Personal or Corporate). A dot on the icon means a filter is on |
| **View** | Switches between **Folder view** and a flat list of every item — see [Switching Between Folders & Flat View](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/password-list-switching-between-folders-flat-view-1) |
| **+** (Add New) | Creates a folder, secret, password or file — see [Creating Items and Generating Passwords](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/pwm-mobile-creating-items) |

In the Recycle Bin, a trash icon in the header empties the bin.

## Searching

Tap the **Search** field, type, and tap **Search** on the keyboard. The search runs across
the whole area, not only the folder you are in, and the results include both items and
folders — tap a folder in the results to open it. Tap **✕** to clear the search, or close it
to return to the list.

For what search matches, see
[Searching for Passwords and Secrets](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/using-search).

## Folders

In folder view, tap a folder to open it. A breadcrumb trail above the list shows where you
are:

- Tap the **home** icon to go back to the top of the area.
- Tap any folder in the trail to jump back to it.
- On a deep path, the middle folders collapse behind **…** — tap it to choose from the full
  list.
- Tap **Back** in the header to go up one level.

Folders with a lock badge are delete-protected.

## Refreshing and scrolling

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Pull down** on the list | Reloads the current area from Akeyless |
| **Back to top** | A button that appears once you scroll down a long list. Tap it to jump to the top |
