Akeyless Platform uses a certified cryptographic module (when the [FIPS mode](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/fips-mode) is enabled on the Gateway) according to National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) FIPS 140-2 by the Cryptographic Module Validation Program (CMVP).
